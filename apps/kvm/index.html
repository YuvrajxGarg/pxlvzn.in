Features

Two computers. One desk.

Kvm makes your Mac and your PC feel like one computer with two screens.

Push past the edge

Arrange your computers in Kvm the way they sit on your desk, then push the pointer past that edge. Every pointing device crosses the same way, and the keyboard always goes with the pointer.

TrackpadMX MasterAny mouseMedia keysCursor lockHold to cross

Admin prompts and the sign-in screen

On Windows, the setup installs the Kvm service, so your Mac’s keyboard and mouse also work on UAC prompts, the lock screen and the sign-in screen after a restart. Ctrl+Alt+Del works too.

One clipboard

Copy on one computer, paste on the other. Text and pictures, both ways.

Send files

Drop files or folders on a computer in the Kvm window and they land in a Kvm folder in its Downloads.

Shortcuts feel native

⌘C on the Mac is Ctrl+C on the PC. Ring, lock or sleep the other computer from Kvm.

⌘ C → Ctrl C

Its screen, sound and camera

See and use the other computer’s screen in a window, hear what it plays, and use this microphone there. The PC can be an extra display for the Mac, and on Windows 11 the Mac’s camera works as a webcam in Zoom, Teams and the rest.

ScreenSoundMicrophoneExtra displayWebcam

Your Android phone too

Scan a code to pair it. Its notifications show up on your computer, the clipboard goes both ways, photos and files go either way with Share › Kvm, and either one can ring the other.

NotificationsClipboardPhotos and filesRing
How it works

Pair once. Then just push.

Open Kvm on both computers, on the same network. That’s the only setup.

Open Kvm on both

They find each other on your network. If yours blocks that, add one by address.

Pair with a code

Click Pair on one computer and type the 6-digit code the other one shows.

Put it where it sits

Drag the other computer to its side of this one. Push the pointer over that edge.

Private by design

It runs on your computer. That's it.

Kvm has no servers and no account. Your computers talk to each other directly on your own network, and every key you press is encrypted with a key only they share. Pairing uses SPAKE2, so nobody on your Wi-Fi can guess the code offline.
