Features

Fast and private. It just finds things.

Your files go straight from one device to the other, over your own network.

Finds your devices

Hop discovers your other computers on the same Wi-Fi with Bonjour (mDNS). No IP addresses, no setup.

MacWindowsiPhoneAndroid

Paired, then encrypted

Pair two devices once with a 6-digit code. The handshake (SPAKE2) means someone else on the Wi-Fi can't guess or intercept it, and every transfer after that is end-to-end encrypted.

482913

Phones, no app

Scan the QR code on your computer and Hop opens in your phone's browser. Send photos either way.

Resumes, then verifies

Big files go in chunks that pick up where they left off if the Wi-Fi drops, with a SHA-256 check at the end.

And a CLI

Send from scripts, servers and SSH sessions.

$ hop send ./renders
How it works

Open it on both. Drop a file.

There's nothing to sign in to and nothing to configure.

Open Hop

On both computers, or scan the QR code with your phone.

Pair once

Type the 6-digit code. After that, your devices trust each other.

Drop and go

Drag files or folders onto a device. They arrive at full Wi-Fi speed.

Private by design

It runs on your computer. That's it.

Hop is the cloud you don't need. Files go straight between your devices over your own network, encrypted, and never touch a server.
