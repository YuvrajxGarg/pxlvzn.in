Features

Clear the junk. Keep what matters.

Dsk knows what rebuilds itself and what doesn’t, and it never ticks your own files for you.

Three honest groups

Safe to clear: caches, logs, build files, package caches. Look first: old installers, stale node_modules, big files you haven’t opened in months. Leave alone: your Photos library, iCloud, the system.

App cachesXcodenpm · pip · cargoBrowser cachesOld installersnode_modules

Ask Claude

Claude reviews every item and says what’s safe, with a one-line reason, or explains a folder you don’t recognise. It only sees paths, sizes and dates, never your files, and uses your own API key.

Trash first, always

Everything Dsk removes goes to the Trash or Recycle Bin, so you can put it back. Your home, Documents and Library folders can never be removed whole.

Seconds with WizTree

On Windows, Dsk uses WizTree when it’s installed to read the whole drive in seconds. On a Mac it measures your folders on several threads.

And a CLI

Clear caches from a script or a cron job.

$ Dsk clean
How it works

Look, tick, clear. Done.

Dsk starts measuring the moment it opens. There’s nothing to set up.

See the bar

Dsk measures your disk and fills the bar by category as it goes.

Check the list

Safe things are ticked for you. Everything else waits for your say, with Claude’s view if you want it.

Move to Trash

One button. Empty the Trash when you’re happy, and the space is yours.

Private by design

It runs on your computer. That's it.

Dsk has no servers. It measures and cleans on your own computer. If you ask Claude, only the list of paths, sizes and dates is sent, with your own key.
