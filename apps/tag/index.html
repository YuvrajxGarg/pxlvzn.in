tag

Take the AI tags off your photos and videos.

AI tools stamp their output with metadata, and platforms read it. Used AI for one VFX pass, a cleanup or an extend? Tag removes those stamps from your photos and videos without touching a single pixel, on your own computer.

Features

Every tag found. Every pixel kept.

Tag rewrites the file's wrapper, not its picture. What you made stays exactly as you made it.

Sees every tag

C2PA Content Credentials, IPTC “made with AI” labels, XMP edit history and the generator stamps from the tools you use.

HiggsfieldFireflyOpenAIMidjourneyRunwayKling

Lossless, bit for bit

Images are rewritten at the container level and videos are remuxed with stream copy, so nothing is re-encoded. Colour profiles, orientation, transparency and HDR stay put.

Photos and videos

JPG, PNG, WebP, MP4, MOV, MKV and WebM. Drop hundreds of files or a whole folder at once.

Originals untouched

Clean copies go to a cleaned folder beside each file, or anywhere you choose. The originals are never modified.

And a CLI

Clean a whole export folder from a script.

$ Tag clean ./exports
How it works

Drop, see, clean. That’s it.

No settings to learn. ffmpeg comes bundled, so there's nothing else to install.

Drop files

Drag photos, videos or whole folders onto the window.

See the tags

Each file lists what it carries: Firefly, Higgsfield, C2PA manifests, IPTC AI labels and more.

Clean

Tag writes clean copies and strikes through every tag it removed.

Private by design

It runs on your computer. That's it.

Tag never uploads your files. It reads and rewrites them on your own machine, so unreleased work stays unreleased.
